Privacy Policy for Funny Tractor

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Funny Tractor ("the App") handles information when you use the game.

Funny Tractor is a children's game designed to provide a simple and entertaining tractor-driving experience.

1. Information We Collect

Funny Tractor does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Camera access
  • Microphone access
  • Health information
  • Payment information

The App does not use personal information to identify individual users.

2. Information Stored on the Device

Funny Tractor may store certain game-related information locally on the user's device, such as:

  • Game score
  • Maximum score
  • Game progress and settings required for the game to function

This information is used only to provide the game's functionality.

This information is stored locally on the device and is not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

3. Children's Privacy

Funny Tractor is designed for young children and families.

We take children's privacy seriously. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The App does not require children to provide personal information, create accounts, communicate with other users, or share personal information.

4. Advertising

Funny Tractor does not display advertisements.

The App does not use advertising identifiers or collect information for personalized advertising.

5. Analytics and Tracking

Funny Tractor does not use analytics services, tracking technologies, or advertising trackers to monitor users across apps or websites.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not share personal information with third parties.

If third-party services or SDKs are added to the App in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe their data practices before they are used.

7. Data Security

Because Funny Tractor does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal information stored on our servers.

Locally stored game data remains on the user's device and is managed by the operating system and the App.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Funny Tractor does not maintain personal user accounts or personal information on developer-controlled servers.

Locally stored game data can be removed by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data through the device's operating system settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published at the same Privacy Policy URL, with a new "Last updated" date.
